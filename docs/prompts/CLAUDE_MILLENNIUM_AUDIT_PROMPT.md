#### 2. The Koszul Young Flattening Operator (Landsberg & Ottaviani 2011)
For $p = \lfloor m/4 \rfloor$, the Koszul Young Flattening $\mathcal{K}_p(T)$ is the exterior algebra linear map:
$$\mathcal{K}_p(T): \bigwedge^p \mathbb{C}^m \otimes \mathbb{C}^{2^{\lfloor m/2 \rfloor}} \longrightarrow \bigwedge^{p+1} \mathbb{C}^m \otimes \mathbb{C}^{2^{\lceil m/2 \rceil}}$$
